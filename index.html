Build Plan

Ingest Checklist

  1. 01 Run the Clinton Digital Library harvester and review any blocked, missing-date, or missing-PDF records.
  2. 02 Assign each record to Volume XXII and its date-band policy cross-reference, XXIII or XXIV.
  3. 03 Reconcile item-level source notes to FRUS-style archival paths using the visible PDF cover sheet and collection trail.
  4. 04 Split regional records into country, NATO/EU, Balkans, Central Europe, and cross-volume review sections.
  5. 05 Track withheld, partial, duplicate, and later-opened records in the queue filter before drafting a documentary chronology.
